He waved, but you never saw him  
He was hoping you would notice  
But it was alright with him that you didn't  
You were focused on other things,  
On life's busy path with enumerous distractions  
All seeming important then,  
But now you can't remember them.  
So he waved, and watched you pass by;  
And though for a moment he was dismayed, his immediate thought —  
"I can't wait for the next chance I get to see him,  
  even if he doesn't see me."  
And so for your whole life,  
He kept waving, kept preparing, kept himself occupied with whether you were happy.  
  
There's a million things you won't remember  
A million things that when reminded of,  
You won't know happened at all:  
A thousand dinners interrupting your plans  
Enumerous car rides and short conversations  
Eyes briefly meeting with a beautiful girl,  
Whom you tried your best to talk to;  
All these things —  
They make up a part of me,  
But I can barely remember them at all  
No library could hold them,  
  they weren't meant to be remembered,  
      they just change anyway . . .  
I must be more than what this life has been  
  
There's rose petals tucked between pages  
You don't know how long they've been there, or who placed them  
They've dried up, still colorful and bright,  
You can tell they weren't placed there yesterday  
How beautiful it can be to not remember  
  
And there's that part of the day,  
A brief window somewhere between the closing of the afternoon and nighttime  
Where orange light trickles in, the kitchen lights are on,  
The underside of the countertops project long shadows to the tile floor  
Someone's preparing dinner, you can tell throughout the house  
As you sit in the next room over, not knowing the love preparing it  
  
I'm afraid of that moment now  
Not because I was afraid of it then,  
But because I'm afraid of that same moment arriving  
Alone and without love  
  
I think it's impossible, it must be  
To know what happened before, and now what happens after  
  
When was the last time?  
A cathartic revelation uncovered a burden that had been held there, more closely than you knew  
The anguish, impossibility of being aware in so many moments,  
No artifacts will be buried to remember them,  
That it's impossible to remember  
  
How can you remember anything anyway,  
It's all moving at a pace you can't comprehend  
Is it a fleeting task to try and make sure you keep track of the important stuff?  
Because what will only happen is that you'll look back and realize you got it all wrong  
  
But even though you cared about many of the wrong things,  
By mercy your heart was still shaped and molded  
By mercy, by grace, the Lord gave you no more than you ever needed  
  
That God preserved you, held you, watched over you  
  
That the one constant in all that's changing all that you can't remember all that you've forgotten all that  
you gave up all that you clutched tightly because it must have meant something all that you refused because  
you didn't want it to mean anything  
  
Was God there?  
He is  
  
I thank You for this life  
This life I can't comprehend  
That You've always been here  
That I didn't have to remember everything, I don't have to despair of what I will lose, what I've lost  
For what will I gain but everything  
Even though so many were good and so many I loved  
  
It's like you're grieving something, it's harrowing  
Like a part of you died with an unfinished uttering  
It would have been profound, if only you had listened  
So listen closely  
or you might miss it,  
   you might miss it  
   you might have to let it go —  
  
Do I really believe I am more than what I can remember?  
That I am more than what this life has been?
